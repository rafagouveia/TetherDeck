# Community conduct

TetherDeck welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Help others participate in English, Portuguese or Spanish when possible.

- Be respectful and discuss the work with concrete evidence.
- Give actionable feedback and allow room for mistakes and questions.
- Do not harass, threaten, insult or target people based on personal characteristics.
- Do not publish private information, credentials, device identifiers or screen
  content belonging to another person.
- Do not use issues or pull requests for spam or unsolicited promotion.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, limit
participation or block repeated abuse. Decisions should be proportionate to the
behavior and protect participants' privacy.

Report a concern to repository maintainers through GitHub's available reporting
or moderation tools. If a private repository reporting channel is available, use
it for sensitive details; do not post another person's private information in a
public issue. No email address is required to participate.

This guide applies to the project's repository, issues, pull requests and enabled
Discussions. It does not authorize the project to collect private account or device data.
